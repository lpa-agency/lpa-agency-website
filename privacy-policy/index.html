2. Hosting and server logs

When you visit the website, GitHub necessarily receives technical connection data, in particular your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested and information about your browser. GitHub uses this data to deliver the website and to keep its service secure, and may store it for a limited period for that purpose.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing a reliable and secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) UK GDPR / GDPR). GitHub may process data in the United States. GitHub relies on recognised transfer mechanisms for this, including the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension as well as standard contractual clauses. Further information is available in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We do not have access to visitor statistics and do not combine this data with any other data.

3. Contacting us by email

If you email us, we process the information you send (for example your name, email address, company and the content of your message) in order to answer your enquiry and, where relevant, to prepare a proposal or contract.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) UK GDPR / GDPR where your enquiry relates to a potential or existing contract, and otherwise our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Our email is provided by Google Workspace (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and Google LLC, USA), which acts as our processor under a data processing agreement. Transfers to the United States are covered by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, its UK Extension and standard contractual clauses.

We delete enquiries once they have been dealt with and no longer need to be kept. Correspondence that leads to a contract is retained for as long as statutory retention obligations require (in the UK generally six years after the end of the financial year concerned).

4. Cookies, analytics and tracking

This website does not use cookies, local storage for tracking, analytics tools, social media plugins, tracking pixels or any advertising technology. For this reason, no cookie banner is shown and no consent is requested.

Testimonial videos are stored on this website and only load when you press play. No third-party video player is used.

5. Links to social media

Our footer links to our profiles on Instagram and LinkedIn. These are ordinary links: no data is sent to these platforms when you view our website. Only if you click a link will you leave our website, and the privacy policy of the respective platform will then apply.
